Quote-ready, defensible

"Couro reads body movement from a single phone camera at publication-grade validity (CCC 0.83–0.94) on two joint measurements — lumbar trunk extension and hip adduction — measured against gold-standard motion capture using the same Bland-Altman framework the OpenCap and Theia3D papers report under. Comparable to 8-camera Theia3D (0.85–0.97) and outperforming 2-phone OpenCap (0.79–0.91) on the strongest slot — from one phone instead of two or eight, with no calibration setup."

What we deliberately don't claim

  • This is not a universal-coverage claim. Three deploy slots are validated today by the criteria above. Our deploy table has 23 slots total; the other 20 are explicitly flagged as "Moderate" or "Not yet validated" rather than reported as accurate.
  • The hip-adduction 0.94 is real but caveated. Its left/right mirror twin scored 0.21 in the same study, which is consistent with partial overfit to our drop-jump validation cohort. We disclose this anywhere the 0.94 appears.
  • Rear-view camera validation is preliminary. Pose detection works geometrically at 180° (Pearson r 0.983 vs mocap), but the only real-data rear-view dataset we've tested against is academic-license-restricted, so we can't quote those numbers commercially. A commercial-clean rear-view validation pipeline shipped today and produces validated rear numbers in next sprint.
  • n = 9–12 subjects per slot. Bland-Altman + CCC + 95% Fisher Z confidence intervals are reported throughout. The ankle slot's CI is wide because n=9 — we say so, and the LOSO cohort is expanding.
