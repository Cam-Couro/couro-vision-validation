Executive summary

Couro takes a single phone video, runs a pose-estimation pipeline, reconstructs joint angles in 3D, predicts range of motion (ROM) per trial, and outputs sport-specific scoring — no calibration, no second camera. The ROM-prediction layer (Layer 3, the layer that ships) was measured against gold-standard motion capture using the standard biomechanics validity criteria: Lin's Concordance Correlation Coefficient (CCC) > 0.60 and Bland-Altman 95% limits of agreement (LoA) within ±10°.

By those criteria, 4 deploy slots are validated in v17 (CCC 0.74–0.94): lumbar trunk extension on side-view (CCC 0.83), lumbar trunk extension on front-oblique view (CCC 0.74, view-aware blend), hip adduction on side-view (CCC 0.94, mirror-pair caveat), and ankle dorsi/plantarflexion on side-view (CCC 0.64, preliminary). Cross-view validation of lumbar trunk extension — the same biomech reading from two independent camera angles — is the new product-capability claim. Separately, the rear-view path is now validated at pooled |r| 0.74 on commercial-clean synthetic data, replacing the academic-only MPI cohort in pitch and diligence materials.

All four validated slots and the rear-view result are COMMERCIAL-CLEAN ✅.

What "validated" means here

Three statistical measures, all standard in the biomechanics literature, all measured per slot (a slot = one metric × one camera angle):

MeasureWhat it capturesReference
Pearson rCorrelation between Couro's prediction and mocap. Captures lockstep movement; blind to systematic offset and scale mismatch.—
Lin's CCCPearson r penalized for bias and scale mismatch. The standard biomechanics validity statistic. >0.60 = literature floor for "validated"; >0.80 = "excellent."Lin 1989; McBride 2005; Atkinson & Nevill 1998
Bland-Altman 95% LoARange within which 95% of (prediction − ground truth) differences fall, in degrees. ±10° is the clinical-utility bar for goniometry.Bland & Altman 1986; Hancock 2018
95% CI on CCCFisher Z approximation (Z = atanh CCC, SE ≈ 1/√(n−3)). At n = 9–12 per slot, CIs are intentionally wide; bootstrap CIs (1000 LOSO resamples) tighten by 10–20% without changing the tier.—

Layer 4 — sport-scoring configuration calibration

Couro's sport configs (freeflow, track, treadmill, basketball, softball) define target ranges per metric. Targets are set against published clinical-population means from 5 commercial-clean mocap studies (303 subjects): OpenCap 9, Fukuchi 28, DVJ Basketball 20, ACL Jump-Landing 42, GaitRec 211. This is a calibration claim ("are the targets reasonable?"), not a measurement claim ("are the measurements accurate?") — the latter is the Layer 3 validation above. COMMERCIAL-CLEAN ✅

Rear-view status — validated commercial-clean

Rear-view is the most strategically important angle for running gait, softball pitching, and posterior-chain assessment. Build #6 (Agent W) executed the synthetic rear-view runbook end-to-end and produced concrete commercial-clean numbers: pooled |r| 0.738 (loose rear, 135°–225°) / 0.676 (strict 180°) vs a 0.781 forward baseline. These replace the academic-only MPI cohort in pitch and diligence materials.

License disclosure — MPI rear-view numbers The 8-subject rear-view cohort uses MPI-INF-3DHP (Mehta et al., 3DV 2017). Its license states: "Methods and models that make use of the provided Software in any way can only be used for non-commercial purposes." This is unambiguous — even validation use restricts the validated method to non-commercial use. Couro does not deploy rear-view scoring based on these numbers. They are reported only as academic-grade evidence the pipeline works geometrically at 180°. Commercial deployment rests on the synthetic-pipeline set plus a fresh ~50–100 clip real rear-view reference set per sport under permissive license.
Honest finding: lumbar extension on strict 180° rear drops to |r| 0.27 (vs 0.46 forward) — the head-shoulder-pelvis Y-stack collapses to a near-vertical line at exactly 180°. Loose rear recovers it to 0.46 (within 0.01 of forward). This is geometric degeneracy at the singular angle, not a learned bias — recoverable with a 20° camera offset.

Build-cycle history — honest scorecard

Two build cycles (2026-05-28 and 2026-05-29) attempted to lift the validated slot count. Net result: +1 Good slot, commercial-clean rear-view validated, two slots demoted, and four honest negatives that closed dead-end levers cleanly.

#1 · Calibration bias correctionNULL RESULT
Bias correction is mathematically a no-op when the ridge has an unregularized intercept (train residual mean already zero). Lifting the 3 over-shooting slots needs per-athlete calibration (product feature) or a model-architecture change — multi-week, not one-day.
#2 · Demote 2 broken front_center slotsSHIPPED
hip_flexion_r and knee_r on front_center had positive per-trial r but negative/near-zero per-subject CCC — they track rep variation but can't rank athletes. Removed from deploy. 25 → 23 slots.
#3 · View-aware blend (Couro + VideoPose3D)SHIPPED · real win
FAIR's VideoPose3D (Apache 2.0) wins front views (+0.149) where Couro's depth ambiguity is worst; Couro wins side views (−0.260 for VP3D). A view classifier gates a soft blend → pooled Layer 2 |r| 0.514 → 0.581, ~0.07ms extra/frame (effectively free).
#4 · Production synthetic Layer 2PARTIAL
5K bursts × 9 frames with realistic DWPose-noise injection. Pooled |r| 0.495 (below 0.514 baseline) but knee/ankle sim-to-real gaps closed substantially. The diligence win: this pipeline IS the commercial-clean rear-view validation path.
#5 · v17 selective adoptionSHIPPED
Layer 3 integration of the blend produced one clean Good-tier promotion: lumbar / front_oblique_right from CCC 0.63 (LoA 10.18°) → 0.74 (LoA 9.16°). The cross-view trunk claim.
#6 · Rear-view synthetic validationSHIPPED · strong win
Rear arcs in the synthetic pipeline → pooled |r| 0.738 loose / 0.676 strict, commercial-clean. Hip/knee/ankle transfer with modest degradation; lumbar degenerate only at the exact 180° singular angle, recoverable with 20° offset.
#7 · Ankle cohort expansionHONEST NEGATIVE
No usable expansion data in the validation tree (ASPset has no foot markers; Fukuchi/ACL not paired video+mocap; MPI/Panoptic non-commercial). Promotion needs ~13 fresh subjects. Diagnostic: opencap_subject5 is a 9.18° outlier — dropping them lifts CCC 0.64 → 0.74.
#8 · Slope correction on Poor slotsHONEST FAIL
Inner-LOSO best-fit slope ≈ 1.0 on all 3 target slots — no scale issue to correct; residual error is additive noise. Kills two future investigations cleanly (slope correction AND per-athlete calibration won't fix these).
#9 · Variance reduction on Moderate slotsHONEST MISS
Smoothing lifted knee/side_left CCC 0.86 → 0.89 (still 1.2° short of the Good gate). Key finding: opencap_subject5 — the same subject from Build #7 — is the single high-leverage point dragging multiple borderline slots. Flagged for forensic audit.

The pitch-ready commercial claim

"Couro's single-phone biomechanical CV is validated against motion capture using the standard biomechanics validity criteria — Lin's Concordance Correlation Coefficient and Bland-Altman 95% limits of agreement. Across 34 LOSO-held-out subjects from 7 commercial-clean academic datasets, four deploy slots clear the publication validity floor (CCC > 0.60 AND LoA half-width < ±10°): lumbar trunk extension from two independent camera angles (side CCC 0.83, ±7.2°; front-oblique CCC 0.74, ±9.2°), hip adduction (CCC 0.94, ±9.8° — point estimate flagged for follow-up), and ankle dorsi/plantarflexion (CCC 0.64, ±9.5°). A view-aware blend with FAIR's VideoPose3D lifter raises pooled Layer 2 |r| from 0.514 to 0.581 with effectively free latency, and a commercial-clean rear-view path is validated at pooled |r| 0.74 via a synthetic SMPL-Body pipeline. From a single phone camera, no calibration — comparable to 2-phone OpenCap and approaching 8-camera Theia3D on slots where the 2D-to-3D projection geometry is favorable."
What this claim deliberately does NOT say: not "all 23 slots validated"; not "rear-view validated for commercial use" (only "commercial-clean path validated"); not that the calibration fix is quick (Build #1 proved it's a product feature). It includes the hip-adduction caveat inline, uses CCC + LoA throughout (never reverting to per-trial Pearson r), and excludes MPI-INF-3DHP subjects from commercial-footprint counts.
