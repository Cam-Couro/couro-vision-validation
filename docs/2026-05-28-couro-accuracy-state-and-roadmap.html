Couro Biomech Accuracy — Where we are, and how to push it higher

2026-05-28 · Internal exec summary · v14 ensemble shipping (RTMPose + DWPose) · 26 subjects · 1,680 trials · LOSO cross-validated

One sentence: 24 of 25 (metric × view) deploy slots ship with zero broken predictions; ceiling correlation with Vicon mocap is 0.85 from a single phone camera — comparable to 8-camera commercial lab systems.

Where we're strong: hip flexion and knee flexion from any of the 5 validated angles (r = 0.70–0.85). These are the metrics softball pitching, hockey, and ACL screening lean on most.

Where we're modest: ankle dorsiflexion (r = 0.41–0.62 from useful angles) and hip adduction from the right side (r = 0.15). Both are data-limited, not algorithm-limited.

Rear-view validation is the honest gap: we have zero rear-camera validation data. Two paths to close it — both proposed below.

Algorithm headroom without new data: 14 levers identified, ranked by expected r-lift × effort. Top tier (5 free quick wins) could lift modest slots by +0.05–0.15 in a week of dev work.

3. Rear-view validation — the honest gap and proposed plan

OpenCap LabValidation and ASPset-510 between them give us 5 camera angles: side_left, front_oblique_left, front_center, front_oblique_right, side_right. Neither dataset has rear-facing cameras. So we have zero rear-view validation today.

For softball pitching specifically (Cameron's project memory), rear views are the natural angle for catching shoulder external rotation, arm path, and trunk twist — metrics that don't show up cleanly from the front. So this is a real gap, not a nitpick.

3a. What we expect rear views to look like (informed guess)

By symmetry of the human body's sagittal plane, rear-center should be similar in difficulty to front-center, and rear-oblique similar to front-oblique. So the expected accuracy is:

MetricRear center (expected)Rear-oblique (expected)
Hip flexion~0.75 (similar to front_center)~0.80
Knee flexion~0.65–0.70~0.75
Hip adduction~0.60~0.55
Lumbar extension~0.50~0.55
Ankle dorsiflexion<0.15 (geometric limit, same as front)~0.30

These are educated guesses, not validated numbers. We should not sell rear-view accuracy until we measure it.

3b. Proposed plan to close the rear-view gap

  1. Cheap path (~$0): mirror our existing side-view models for rear use. Anatomical symmetry says a side-right camera and a rear-camera see roughly the same body axis. Ship these as "preliminary rear models" with clear disclaimer.
  2. Validated path (~$2–4K + 2 weeks): collect 1–2 subjects through OpenCap Cloud (Stanford's mocap-rental service) with 8 cameras, including 2 rear-facing. Use that to confirm rear-view models match expectations. Cheaper than full mocap collection because we only need 1–2 subjects to verify the symmetry assumption.
  3. Sport-specific path (longer): piggyback rear-view validation onto the AUSL softball mocap collection if/when that happens. Adds zero marginal cost.

4. The algo upgrade menu (no new data required)

14 levers we have not pulled yet, ranked by expected r-lift × effort. None require new validation data; all are pure software work on the existing pipeline.

Tier 1 — Free quick wins (1–3 days each, expected +0.02–0.05 per lift)

#LeverExpected r-liftEffortWhat it does
1Soft-argmax / DARK keypoint decoding+0.02–0.04 all slots1 day Replace integer argmax on SimCC heatmaps with continuous expectation. Free sub-pixel precision from existing ONNX outputs.
2Temporal smoothing of keypoints+0.03–0.05 all slots1 day Savitzky-Golay or 1-pole IIR before feature extraction. Cuts frame-to-frame keypoint jitter (we measured 1.1–1.2 px) by ~50%.
3Per-slot ridge regularization (alpha CV)+0.02–0.05 modest slots1 day Currently alpha=10 hardcoded across all 25 slots. Cross-validating alpha per slot will help small-n slots specifically.
4Polynomial cross-terms in features+0.02–0.03 all slots2 days Currently linear in features. Add target × coupling interactions. Captures known biomech non-linearities.
5Use user-provided height when available+0.01–0.03 r, -10–15% RMSE1 day Production already collects height; we don't currently feed it to anthropometric reconstruction.

Tier 1 combined could realistically deliver +0.05–0.15 r on modest slots (ankle, hip adduction side_right, etc.) in roughly one week of focused dev work. Strong slots already near 0.85 will see less benefit.

5. The structural ceiling — what won't move

Honesty matters here. Three slots are at or near an information-theoretic limit:

SlotCurrent rWhy it's a ceiling
Ankle × front_oblique_right+0.02 Right ankle hidden behind right hip from this angle. Geometric occlusion — no algorithm sees through bodies.
Ankle × front_center+0.15 Foot points at the camera; dorsiflexion produces almost zero pixel motion. Information-theoretic.
Hip adduction × side_right+0.15 Frontal-plane motion seen sagittally → perpendicular to image plane. Visible motion is just camera noise.

These can be addressed only by (a) a second camera at an orthogonal angle, (b) IMU fusion, or (c) "don't offer this combination" — guide users to a different angle for these metrics. Algorithm work won't move them.

The general r=0.85 ceiling on the best slots is also somewhat structural — Theia3D (8 cameras) gets 0.85–0.95. We are at 0.85 from 1 camera. Pushing past r=0.90 from 1 camera likely needs 2-camera fusion.

6. Suggested order (not committed sprint — just an order)

  1. This week: Tier 1 levers #1–5. Cheap, should push modest slots up by +0.05–0.15 collectively. Most likely to move ankle from 0.41 → ~0.50 and hip adduction side_right from 0.15 → ~0.25.
  2. Next week: Tier 2 levers #6–8 (ensemble, wider couplings, CNN event detector). These touch event-anchored slots specifically.
  3. Validation in parallel: rear-view OpenCap Cloud collection (1–2 subjects, $2–4K, 2 weeks). Closes the rear-view gap concurrently.
  4. Then decide: if Tier 1+2 closes the modest slots to ≥0.50, we may not need Tier 3. If they don't, Tier 3 #12 (synthetic AMASS data) is the next big bet.
  5. Don't yet: sport-specific mocap collection. Costs $5–10K and takes weeks. Should wait until algorithm levers are exhausted AND there's a paying customer driving the spec.
