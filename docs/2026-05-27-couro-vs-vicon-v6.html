Couro vs Vicon — final deploy-ready models

v6 · 2026-05-27 · OpenCap LabValidation drop jumps · best of {full-trial, event-anchored} per slot · LOSO cross-validated

Final tally: 19 of 25 (metric × camera-view) slots are deploy-ready. The remaining 5 are all ankle dorsiflexion variants — a known camera-angle / keypoint-occlusion limit, not a feature-engineering one.

Recipe. For each slot, we trained both approaches and picked the winner by LOSO Pearson r: (a) full-trial ROM features (v4), or (b) DJ-impact event-anchored loading-window features (v6). 11 slots picked full-trial, 14 picked event-anchored.

Biggest gains from event-anchoring: hip flexion side-right went from no signal (r=−0.03) to strong (r=+0.50). Lumbar extension front-center went from scale-only (r=0.07) to strong (r=+0.53). Knee flexion side-right went from r=0.55 to r=0.64.

3. Why some slots picked event-anchored, others picked full-trial

The split isn't random — there's a clear biomech pattern:

  • Event-anchored wins when the metric's signal lives during ground contact and the full-trial ROM dilutes it with airborne motion. Lumbar extension on front views is the cleanest example — most v4 "scale-only" cases collapsed because the trunk wobbles in flight, drowning out the loading-phase lean that matters.
  • Full-trial wins when the metric's signal is large enough that the airborne portion doesn't dominate, and when event-detection drops too many trials (Cam2 front-center loses ~20% of trials because the right-ankle keypoint is foreshortened dead-on).
  • Both fail for right-ankle dorsiflexion on right-side cameras — this is the camera-occlusion limit, not a feature problem. Right-ankle keypoint is partially occluded by the drop box on Cam3/Cam4.

4. The remaining gap: ankle dorsiflexion

4 of 5 ankle views still won't deploy. The structural diagnosis is solid: right-ankle keypoint quality drops sharply when the camera is on the right side of the subject (Cam3, Cam4) because of the box edge occluding the foot at landing. On front_center (Cam2), foreshortening of the foot pointing at the camera kills the angle measurement.

Three real fixes, in order of effort:

FixEffortLikely outcome
View-aware foot-side selection — measure right-ankle from cameras on subject's left only (and vice versa)~1 day4 of 5 ankle views become testable; final coverage TBD
Foot-vector from midpoint(big_toe, small_toe) − heel instead of heel → big_toe (2-keypoint averaging cuts noise √2×)~½ dayModest noise reduction — won't fix occlusion but helps front_center
Multi-camera fusion at inference — triangulate when 2+ camera angles available~1 month (real eng project)Big — pushes ankle into deploy zone even on the worst single views

Code shape per metric (mirrors the existing knee_rotation.py pattern):

class HipFlexionROM(MetricCalculator):
    MODELS = load_json("deploy_ready_models.json")["models"]["hip_flexion_r"]

    def calculate(self, keypoint_series, camera_view):
        model = self.MODELS[camera_view]      # picks side_left, front_center, etc.

        if model["approach"] == "event_anchored":
            events = detect_dj_events(keypoint_series.ankle_y, keypoint_series.fps)
            features = extract_event_anchored_features(keypoint_series, events)
        else:
            features = extract_full_trial_features(keypoint_series)

        z = (features - model["feature_mean"]) / model["feature_std"]
        return float(z @ model["weights_zscored"] + model["intercept"])

6. What's still worth investing in

  1. Ship the 19 deploy-ready models via PR to couro-vision. Pattern matches existing knee_rotation.py exactly — ~1 week for Saad. This is the immediate commercial value.
  2. View-aware foot-side selection — small change, potentially unlocks 4 of the 5 broken ankle slots.
  3. Validate beyond drop-jumps — current numbers are DJ-only. The AUSL / Cal Berkeley softball-pitching opportunity and Sharks hockey-skating opportunity need their own validation runs against sport-specific datasets. AthletePose3D (Waseda) is the obvious candidate; needs commercial licensing conversation.
  4. Multi-camera fusion — when an athlete records 2+ angles (already standard for softball), triangulation directly recovers 3D and would lift every metric significantly, especially ankle.
